Before you hire anyone for AI work

The questions
worth asking.

Most AI projects don't fail because the technology can't do it. They fail because nobody agreed on what happens when it's wrong, who owns it, and how it gets handed over.

Here's where I stand — ask any consultant you're considering the same things.

Will this replace my team?
No. I automate the repetitive, judgment-light work so your people spend their hours on customers instead of copy-paste. Anything requiring real judgment keeps a human in the loop.
Where does my data go?
Into your accounts, under your keys. I'll tell you exactly which tools touch which data, and I configure business-tier settings so your content isn't used for training.
What happens when the AI gets it wrong?
Every workflow gets an approval step or a confidence threshold, an alert when it fails, and a documented manual fallback. Nothing customer-facing ships unreviewed by default.
Am I locked into you?
Never. You own the accounts, keys, and documentation from day one, and every build ends with a walkthrough so your team can run it without me.
